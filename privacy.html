Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Surgeon Explains (the Application) is a private tool used by the owner of the Surgeon Explains YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos to that channel.

Data we access

With the owner's explicit authorisation through Google OAuth, the Application uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to, and read basic information about, the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we collect, store and share

The Application does not collect personal data from viewers or any other third party. The only credential stored is the owner's OAuth refresh token, kept in a private, access-controlled environment and used solely to upload the channel's own videos. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and videos can be removed from the channel by its owner at any time.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact the Surgeon Explains channel through its YouTube channel page.
